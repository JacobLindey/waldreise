# Make a critter


## 1. Attributes

Your critter has three attribute scores that measure their basic talents.

- **Daring.** forcefulness, assertiveness, power
- **Grace.** elegance, agility, poise, precision
- **Heart.** charisma, emotional awareness, inner strength

For each of these attributes, in order, roll **3d6**.

You may then swap any two attributes.

## 2. HP, pips, and background

**Roll d6** for your **Hit Protection** (HP). This is the damage your critter can shrug off before taking serious harm.

**Roll d6** for your critter's starting **pips**. These are the basic currency of the world.

**Cross reference** the value of your **HP** with your **pips** on the **Background table**. This is what your critter has been doing up to now.

## 3. Starting gear

Your critter starts with:

- A piece of starting loot
- Up to three pieces of basic equipment
- Up to three weapons of your choice
- Two special possessions from your background

If your critter's highest attribute is 11 or less, roll on the Backgrounds table and take one special possession from that background. If your highest attribute is 8 or less, take two.

## 4. Details

Roll or choose 

Choose a name appropriate for your critter.

### Blood-soaked past

You left behind a life of violence and a name mothers used to scare their children. For whatever reason, the people in town took you (back?) in and treat you like one of their own.

### Sheriff

You keep order in town and protect it from outside threats. It's not be anything official, but everyone knows you've got a cool head and the weight to back up your words.

### Storm-marked

You've been touched by the God of Storms (Rainmaker, Thunderhead, Slayer of Beasts!) and bear runic markings. When did the marks manifest? Are they a symbol of your strengh, speed, and courage? Or their source?
