# Inventory

## Inventory slots

Your critter carries items in their inventory slots. Most items take up one inventory slot. Some larger items, such as two-handed weapons and armor take up two slots.

### Paw slots

Your critter has two paw slots.

Items in these slots are **carried**.

### Body slots

Your critter has two body slots.

Items in these slots are **worn**.

Worn items can be swapped with paw slots as a free action.

### Pack slots

You critter has six pack slots, numbered 1 through 6.

Items in these slots take time to retrieve when under pressure. Roll a d6. Items in pack slots numbered equal or less than the result can be accessed as a free action. All others require an action.

## Conditions

Conditions are **effects** bore by your critter. Each condition must be placed in an inventory slot. Critters may have the same condition multiple times.

Some conditions have additional effects. These effects apply as long as the condition remains in your inventory.

A condition can only be **removed** from your inventory by meeting its **clear** requirement.

## Item usage

Most items have a **usage track**.

As the item sees wear and tear, you'll mark its usage track.

When the usage track of an item is completed, the item becomes useless.

### Tracking use

When asked to check an item's usage, roll d6 for each unmarked use. For each 5 or 6, mark the item's usage track.

An item's entry indicates when to check usage.

- **Activate.** Check usage immediately after the item is used.

- **DT.** Check usage at the end of a dungeon turn in which the item was used.

- **Watch.** Check usage at the end of a watch in which the item was used.

The ref may also ask you to check usage any time an item is misused or threatened.

### All used up

Usage dots can be cleared from some items through repair or rituals. 

- **Destroyed.** The item is permanently useless. It might make a good paperweight.

- **Refuel.** The item's usage track can be cleared by using the indicated fuel. For instance, oil can refuel a lantern.

- **Rest.** The item's usage track is cleared when the critter carrying it completes the indicated rest (short, long, or full).

- **Repair.** The item's usage track can be cleared by repairing it during down time.

- **Ritual.** The item's usage track is cleared when the indicated ritual is performed.

## Load & encumbrance

The number of items and conditions in your critter's inventroy determines how heavy a load they carry.

| Slots Filled | Load       | Effects                    |
|:------------:|:-----------|:---------------------------|
| 0-3          | Light      | quick, quiet               |
| 4-6          | Normal     |                            |
| 7-10         | Heavy      | noisy, slow, quick to tire |
| 11+          | Encumbered | *see below                 |

As long as they aren't encumbered, a critter's load just informs the fiction. "The slope's steep, but with plenty of handholds. It's no problem unless you've got a heavy load -- then you'll be Defying Danger."

While encumbered, your critter cannot run, and makes all rolls with disadvantage.
